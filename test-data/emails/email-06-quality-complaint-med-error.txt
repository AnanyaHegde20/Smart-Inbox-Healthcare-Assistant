Incident Report - Medication Error

Date of Incident: 2025-08-17
Location: City Hospital, Pharmacy Department

Details:
Patient Jane Smith (DOB: 05/22/1975, MRN: 789012) was dispensed Warfarin 5mg instead of the prescribed Warfarin 2.5mg.

The error was discovered during the second dose administration by nurse Patricia Lee.
The patient had received one incorrect dose (5mg instead of 2.5mg) before the error was caught.

Patient Impact:
- INR checked immediately: 3.2 (therapeutic range 2.0-3.0)
- No bleeding events observed
- Vitamin K administered prophylactically
- Patient monitored for 24 hours

Root Cause Analysis:
- Look-alike/sound-alike medications stored adjacent
- Barcode scanning was bypassed due to scanner malfunction
- Pharmacist was covering two stations simultaneously

Corrective Actions:
1. Barcode scanner replaced immediately
2. Staff retrained on look-alike medication protocols
3. Independent double-check for high-alert medications reinforced
4. Physical separation of Warfarin strengths implemented

This incident has been reported to the hospital's Quality Improvement Committee.
