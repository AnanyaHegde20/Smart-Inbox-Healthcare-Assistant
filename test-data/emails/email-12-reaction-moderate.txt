Subject: Adverse Reaction - Ketorolac - Post-Surgical Patient
Date: 2025-09-07T08:45:00Z

Patient Adverse Drug Reaction Report

Patient: Ellen R. Foster (synthetic ID: SYN-3391)
Age: 58, Female
Allergies: NKDA (prior to this event)
Procedure: Right knee arthroscopy, 2025-09-05

Post-Operative Medications:
- Ketorolac 15mg IV q6h (started day of surgery)
- Oxycodone 5mg PO q6h PRN
- Ondansetron 4mg IV q8h PRN

Reaction Onset: Day 2 post-op (2025-09-06)
Time from first dose: Approximately 18 hours

Symptoms:
- Gross hematuria (visible blood in urine)
- flank pain, right side
- Decreased urine output (~200mL in 8 hours)
- Serum creatinine elevated: 1.8 mg/dL (baseline 0.9)

Assessment:
Acute kidney injury likely secondary to ketorolac (NSAID)
in a post-surgical patient with transient hypotension.

Actions Taken:
1. Ketorolac discontinued immediately
2. IV fluid resuscitation started
3. Urine output monitored hourly
4. Nephrology consult placed
5. Creatinine trended every 12 hours

Outcome: Creatinine returned to 1.1 mg/dL by day 4.
Urine cleared by day 3. Patient discharged on day 5
with normal renal function.
