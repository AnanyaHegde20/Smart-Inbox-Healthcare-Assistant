=== EVENT DESCRIPTION ===
Onset Date: 2025-09-01 (Day 7 of celecoxib therapy)
Location: Patient's home, followed by hospital admission

Clinical Course:
Day 7 (2025-09-01): Patient noticed "sunburn-like" rash on
  trunk. Initially attributed to new laundry detergent.

Day 8 (2025-09-02): Rash spread to arms, legs, and face.
  Developed fever (38.9C / 102F). Oral blisters noted.
  Patient went to Emergency Department.

Day 8 ED Assessment:
  - Vital Signs: T 38.9C, HR 102, BP 118/74, RR 18, SpO2 97%
  - Skin: Erythematous maculopapular rash with targetoid lesions
    covering approximately 35% BSA. Multiple oral erosions on
    buccal mucosa and tongue. Two large blisters on forearms.
  - Eyes: Conjunctival injection, bilateral. No corneal erosions.
  - Nikolsky sign: Positive (skin slippage with gentle pressure)

Day 8 Diagnosis: Stevens-Johnson Syndrome (SJS)
  - Severity: SJS (not TEN - BSA involvement <10% with full-thickness)
  - Causality Assessment: Probable (Naranjo score: 8)
  - Suspected trigger: Celecoxib

Day 8-14: Hospital Admission - Burn Unit/ICU
  - Celecoxib discontinued immediately
  - Wound care with silver sulfadiazine dressings
  - IV fluids: Normal saline 125 mL/hr
  - Pain management: Morphine PCA
  - Ophthalmology consult: Artificial tears q2h, no corneal involvement
  - Nutrition: NPO initially, then soft diet when oral lesions improved
  - Infection prevention: Contact precautions, daily wound cultures

Day 10: Skin biopsy results
  - Histopathology: Full-thickness epidermal necrosis with subepidermal
    blister formation. Consistent with SJS.
  - Direct immunofluorescence: Negative

Day 14 (2025-09-08): Discharged to home
  - BSA involvement improved to approximately 10%
  - Oral lesions healing
  - Renal function normal (Cr 0.8 mg/dL)
  - No ocular sequelae
