Medical History:

Event Description:
Patient developed anaphylaxis approximately 15 minutes after receiving first oral dose of Amoxicillin 500mg for dental prophylaxis.

Symptoms:
- Immediate onset urticaria (widespread)
- Angioedema of lips and tongue
- Bronchospasm
- Hypotension (BP dropped from 120/80 to 85/50)
- Tachycardia (HR 110 bpm)

Product Information:
Drug: Amoxicillin
Manufacturer: Global Pharma Inc.
Lot: GP-AMX-2025-112
NDC: 98765-432-10
Dose: 500mg oral
Route: Oral
Indication: Dental prophylaxis

Treatment of Event:
- Epinephrine 0.3mg IM administered immediately
- Diphenhydramine 50mg IV
- Methylprednisolone 125mg IV
- Normal saline 1L IV bolus
- Supplemental oxygen

Outcome: Recovered with sequelae (respiratory symptoms resolved within 4 hours, mild fatigue persisted for 3 days)
