PRODUCT RECALL NOTIFICATION

Recall Reference: PR-2025-0089
Date: September 2, 2025

Product Information:
Product: SterileGuard Surgical Gloves, Size 7.5
SKU: SG-SG-75-2025
Lot Numbers: SGL-2025-0451 through SGL-2025-0478
Manufacturing Date: 2025-06-15
Expiry: 2028-06-14

Reason for Recall:
Routine quality testing identified that a portion of gloves in the above lot numbers fail to meet the ASTM D6319 standard for pinhole testing. Failure rate: 2.3% (acceptable: <0.5%).

This may result in potential exposure to bloodborne pathogens during surgical procedures.

Distribution:
- 45,000 pairs distributed to 28 healthcare facilities
- Primary distribution: Northeast region

Actions Required:
1. Immediately cease use of affected lots
2. Quarantine remaining inventory
3. Contact MedEquip Solutions for return authorization
4. Check inventory for lot numbers listed above

Replacement Product:
Available immediately at no additional cost.
